Profile

My path combines physical and digital infrastructure. I started with electrical systems and automation, then expanded my education into networks, servers, virtualization, containers and infrastructure automation.

I like understanding systems end to end: from power, control and maintenance to connectivity, services, availability and observability. That combination lets me approach technical problems across more than one layer and document the work with an operational mindset.

I also have experience in on-site technical support, independent electrical services and applied networking projects. I keep specialized Electrical and Networks profiles for focused roles; this version is the reference when the complete picture is more useful.
